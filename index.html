StudySync

Our Mission

We, EduPulse, developed StudySync as a way to solve two major problems. Firstly, in private schools and many universities where device usage is a key part of the learning ecosystem, AI is often misused and often serves a purpose in cheating on assignments, unfair means during testing, etc. Secondly, people who live in low-income households often do not have proper access to education, giving people who are financially fortunate an advantage in succeeding academically over those who are not financially fortunate. We want to close this gap between children of low-income households and children of high-income households.

VR Headset Addons

To make learning much more interactive, EduPulse has decided to create VR headset addons, with three main models.

The EduPulse Basic

Features a cardboard structure, a neodymium ring magnet and a ceramic disc magnet to form an input, a headstrap, and biconvex lenses (25 mm diameter, 45 mm focal length) to create a great 3D experience.

AED 4–8INR 86–172

The EduPulse Mobile Pro

Features a polycarbonate body shell with adjustable optic lenses, a premium headstrap, and face cushioning to allow for more comfortable learning. It has a locking mechanism, and 2 inputs can be taken at a time.

AED 34–68INR 800–1,800Price depends on market conditions

The EduPulse Pro

Has its own screen, two controllers supporting up to 5 inputs alongside analog sticks, face cushioning, premium optic lenses, and an audio system to immerse the user in learning. It has an elite cooling and thermal unit, a durable polycarbonate and polyamide structure for maximum durability, a much longer battery life, and an upgrade from a 60 Hz screen to a 90 Hz screen.

From AED 400 (basic version)
Up to AED 799 (8 inputs)
